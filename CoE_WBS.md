# Center of Excellence — End-to-End Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

Companion artifact to the white paper *"Building the Center of Excellence"* and its [companion site](./index.html). This WBS covers the full lifecycle of standing up and running a CoE — from business case through steady-state operation, scaling, and (where applicable) sunset.

**How to read this:** WBS IDs are hierarchical (`1.0` phase → `1.1` work package → `1.1.1` task). Durations are indicative planning guidance for a mid-sized CoE (adjust for scope, resourcing model, and CoE type — an AI/GenAI CoE often compresses Phases 1–3 versus a Program Management CoE). "Owner" refers to the role most accountable, not the only contributor.

## 1.0 Initiation & charter

| WBS ID | Task | Description | Owner | Key deliverable | Indicative duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | **Initiation & charter** | | | | **3–5 weeks** |
| 1.1 | Business case & problem definition | Name the specific business pain point the CoE will address — not a generic ambition | Executive sponsor + prospective CoE lead | One-page problem statement | 1 week |
| 1.1.1 | Identify capability gaps or inconsistency across teams | Interview practice/delivery leads on recurring pain points | Prospective CoE lead | Gap summary | 3 days |
| 1.1.2 | Quantify the cost of inaction | Estimate rework, delay, or risk cost of the status quo | CoE lead + Finance | Cost-of-inaction estimate | 2 days |
| 1.2 | Secure executive sponsorship | Identify and confirm a sponsor with budget authority | Prospective CoE lead | Named sponsor, budget commitment | 1 week |
| 1.3 | Operating model decision | Decide whether a Practice, GCC/GBS, or CoE (or combination) is the right construct | Sponsor + CoE lead | Operating model decision memo | 1 week |
| 1.4 | Draft the charter & vision statement | Who we serve, what excellence looks like, how we get there, by when | CoE lead | Approved charter document | 1 week |
| 1.5 | Initial scope boundary definition | What the CoE owns vs. what stays with delivery teams | CoE lead + sponsor | Scope boundary document | 3 days |

| WBS ID | Task | Description | Owner | Key deliverable | Indicative duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Notes on using this WBS

- **Durations are indicative**, not commitments — an AI/GenAI CoE with an urgent executive mandate may compress Phases 1–3 into weeks; a Program & Portfolio Management CoE spanning many geographies may take longer in Phase 3 (standards development) given the breadth of existing practice to reconcile.
- **Phase 7 is conditional.** Program Management and Data & Analytics CoEs, being foundational disciplines, typically never reach Phase 7 — they right-size (6.2) rather than sunset. AI/GenAI CoEs may be deliberately chartered as a 2–3 year build-then-handoff program.
- **This WBS assumes one CoE.** If standing up multiple CoEs concurrently (as is common for AI and Program Excellence together), share Phase 1–2 stakeholder and governance design work where the stakeholder sets overlap, and stand up the cross-CoE governance layer (6.4) from day one rather than retrofitting it later.
